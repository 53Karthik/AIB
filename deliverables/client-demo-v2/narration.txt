Every service level. One clear story.
Welcome to A I B Life's service governance application. Follow the complete journey, from operational data sources to a clear, evidence based view of performance.

From evidence to governance
Upload your data sources, apply the Schedule twenty three rules, investigate the results, and share a monthly governance pack. Here is how the journey connects.

Start with the whole picture
The dashboard brings reporting periods together. See which service levels missed target, how many policies were measured, and what remains overdue. A shortcut opens the latest governance pack.

Load the evidence once
Start in Data Sources. Upload correspondence, cancellation, withdrawal, and workflow files as CSV or Excel. The application identifies each source from its contents.

Five sources. One reporting picture.
The checklist confirms all five required sources. File details show the records received. The snapshot date and quality notes explain the evidence behind the reporting set.

Every reporting month, rebuilt together
New files replace earlier files in the same slot and rebuild every monthly pack. Analysts can also rebuild on demand, remove files, or reload the supplied data sources.

Choose the period that matters
Choose a reporting month from the dashboard. Search makes older periods easy to find. Incomplete periods are clearly labelled, keeping partial months in context.

Review the SLA Status
Reports provides access to S L A Status, exceptions, and the governance pack. Status compares five headline service levels against target, with met, missed, and overdue counts.

Two rates. Two useful perspectives.
Pass or fail uses completed policies: met divided by met plus missed. The second rate includes overdue open work. Counts represent service records, so a policy can appear more than once.

Trace a percentage to its policies
Select a service level to see its underlying policies. Product, handler, timing, and outcome connect each result to operational evidence. Unit linked performance also expands into three component steps.

Focus on policies that missed
Outcome filters narrow the list immediately. Isolate missed policies and compare the required deadline with actual completion, using the evidence that produced the monthly score.

Separate missed work from overdue work
Exceptions brings failing service levels and failed policies together. Switch between missed deadlines and open overdue work. A service level can pass overall and still contain individual exceptions.

Keep evidence quality in view
Data quality findings flag overdue policies, unmatched approvals, exclusions, and incomplete periods. Reviewers can see where the evidence needs attention before drawing conclusions.

Open the wider performance story
Open Intelligence to explore the reporting history. The latest complete month's status sits alongside recurring misses, performance trends, and outstanding work across the reporting window.

Executive insight with Amazon Nova
Amazon Nova Pro turns the computed report figures into an executive explanation. The summary covers recent performance, the wider reporting window, failure concentrations, and overdue backlog.

Explore performance against target
Monthly trends compare each service level with its target over time. Change the selection to explore another pattern. The service level record summarises complete months below target.

Choose a reporting window
The scope selector narrows the analysis to a chosen month and everything before it. The executive summary refreshes for that window, keeping the governance discussion focused.

See where failures concentrate
Failure concentrations highlight handlers, products, and transaction types with unusually high failure rates. These patterns direct investigation. They show where failures occur, without proving their cause.

Keep outstanding obligations visible
The backlog groups open overdue policies by service level and shows the oldest deadline. This keeps unfinished obligations visible alongside the formal completed policy performance rate.

Ask Nova about the report
Ask a question about the report. Here, Nova explains where non unit linked failures concentrate. The model's answer uses the report's computed evidence, with its source identified beneath the response.

Open the Governance pack
Open Governance pack directly from the month's results. The dashboard also offers a shortcut to the latest complete month. Summary, S L A Status, exceptions, quality findings, and source evidence form one document.

Download a report to share
Select Export as PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the browser's print window. Share the monthly findings with colleagues and clients, supported by the evidence reviewed throughout this journey.
