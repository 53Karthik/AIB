The data source date anchors overdue work

Open workflow creation date + Pending Since Days gives candidate dates; the most common wins. If unavailable, the code falls back to the day after the latest activity. “Overdue” means the deadline day is before this as-of date.

Quality travels alongside the result

Set-wide findings and month-specific findings are attached to the packs. Missing files, unmatched approvals, mapping quirks and partial periods remain visible when the user interprets the numbers.

Evidence suppliedFeedsHow it is used

CANREVEXT cancellations23CProtection cancellations joined to Approve Cancellation workflows.
WITHDRAWALEXT withdrawals23B UL Step 2Withdrawal status joined to its policy's approval workflow.
WRKFLWEXT open + closed23A · UL Steps 2–3 · 23C · 23ECombined workflow population supplies clocks, completions and matching context.

Reporting month follows EBQ receipt or workflow creation. Unmatched withdrawal/cancellation events use their last-status month. Uploading CSV and XLSX versions into the same slots replaces the earlier files; they are not added together.

05 / THE DECISION LOGIC

What “met target” actually means.

Schedule 23 defines the targets. Policy rules determine the counts. The completed-policy rate determines the service-level verdict. Policies are counted per service record; the same policy can appear more than once.

Service levelTargetWhen a policy meets the rule
23A · Policy issue97%Before 15:00: close the same business day. At/after 15:00: close the same or next business day.
23B NUL · Alterations96%Mapped non-unit-linked EBQ policy merged by the end of the next business day after receipt.
23B UL · Unit-linked98%Combined counts from three steps: 1 EBQ merged the same business day; 2 withdrawal completed under the approval workflow's 15:00 rule; 3 unit adjustment workflow closed under the 15:00 rule.
23C · Cancellations96%In-scope protection cancellation: strictly less than 48 elapsed hours from matched approval creation to cancellation last status.
23E · Unrecognised EFT98%Matching Manual Review workflow closed the same or next business day. Description must contain “EFT Payment Not Recognised”.

Rate (completed) → PASS / FAIL

met ÷ (met + missed)

At or above target = PASS. Below target = FAIL. No completed policies = NO_DATA, with no verdict.

Rate including overdue open work

met ÷ (met + missed + open overdue)

Shows the impact of overdue unfinished work. Open policies not yet due, exclusions and unmatched policies do not enter this denominator.

Illustrative record journey · not a live record

Why a next-day completion can still be a miss

23A created Monday, 14:59→Due Monday→Closed Tuesday→MISSED
Assuming Monday and Tuesday are business days. At exactly 15:00, the deadline shifts to Tuesday and that completion meets the rule. For 23C, 47h 59m 30s meets the rule; exactly 48h does not.

Business-day rules skip weekends and configured Irish holidays. A non-business-day start moves to the next business day, before the cutoff. The 23C rule instead compares raw elapsed hours. Rejected EBQ policies are excluded; unmatched approval events are listed without an SLA score.

04 / Checks

Accept or fall back

Reject unsupported numeric figures. The executive narrative also checks selected pass/fail and “most often” claims.

How failure concentrations work

Group measured policies by assignee, EBQ user, product, workflow or transaction type. Compare each group's failure rate with its SLA's rate.

  • Minimum lift: 1.25× the SLA's failure rate.
  • Monthly packs: at least 3 measured policies and 2 failures; up to 8 groups.
  • Intelligence: at least 5 measured policies and 3 failures; up to 10 groups.
  • Rank by excess failures. These are descriptive patterns, not demonstrated causes.

The actual AI boundary

Default model: Amazon Nova Pro through Bedrock; the code also supports an Anthropic model path. Model failures trigger a rules response.

  • Narratives are cached on disk by a hash of the computed brief.
  • Q&A is single-turn, report-scoped, with no retrieval or conversation memory.
  • Q&A currently applies the numeric guard only. It does not invoke the narrative's claim checker.
  • Checks are limited heuristics; they are not a proof that every generated sentence is correct.

Current implementation boundaries

  • File uploads and bundled files provide the data; there is no direct source-system connection.
  • State is stored in files, with one current pack per month. Rebuilds replace prior packs; there is no versioned reporting archive.
  • The API has no application login or role enforcement, and no workflow for assigning or resolving exceptions.
  • Forecasts, service credits and automatic remediation are outside the implemented feature set.

Dataset & verification context

  • The delivered data is documented as synthetic data in real data source formats. It represents a 24 September 2026 snapshot spanning 22 reporting months.
  • The existing verification harness compares CSV and XLSX calculations with SLA_Expected_Results.xlsx.
  • The project log records zero differences and 24 passing tests from the prior implementation verification; those engine checks were not rerun for this guide.
  • Tracked follow-ups: support header-less real EBQ exports and extend the holiday list beyond 3 August 2026.

Persistence detail: imports/rebuilds are queued within the Node process. Rebuild clears and rewrites the monthly files; it is not an atomic switch of the whole dataset. Durable hosting needs a persistent DATA_DIR. The supplied Render blueprint documents ephemeral storage on its free configuration.
